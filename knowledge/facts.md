- **W02.** The proposed hero is a dark **ASCII black hole** with **five colored dust zones**, one for each featured project. [D, pp. 3–5]

- **W06.** The v1 mascot is planned as a **plain small language model** answering questions about Rastin using approved facts provided server-side on each request. **No persona training or model fine-tuning** is planned for v1. [D, pp. 7–8]
- **W07.** The planned site includes **Projects, Papers, Notes, and About** content areas. Its Papers area is for research papers **Rastin has read and summarized**; this does **not** mean he authored or published those papers. Final menu details remain open. [D, p. 9]

- **W09.** The proposed implementation uses **Astro**, with **React and Three.js** for interactive features, **Cloudflare Pages** for the site, and **Cloudflare Workers** for chatbot integration.
